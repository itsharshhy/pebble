# Ethics and claims
*Phase: Safety and ethics · What Pebble may say about itself, and what it may not.*

Mental-health apps are often criticised for overclaiming. Pebble's credibility depends on
saying **less than we could**, and every word being true.

## The claims test
Before any sentence goes into the README, About, a release note, a post or a talk:
1. **Is it backed** by an entry in [Science](../00-foundation/science.md)?
2. **Is the strength** 🟢 or 🟡? (🟠 never appears in claims.)
3. **Is the population right?** (A finding in people with GAD isn't a finding about "everyone".)
4. **Is it about the method, not about Pebble?** We haven't studied Pebble itself, so we describe the research behind the method and never claim results for the app.
5. **Would a sceptical psychologist nod?**

## Allowed vs not allowed
| ✅ Allowed | ❌ Not allowed |
|---|---|
| "Based on the Worry Outcome Journal, a technique studied in anxiety research." | "Clinically proven." |
| "In one study, 91.4% of tracked worries didn't come true." | "91% of your worries will never happen." |
| "Some people find that seeing this evidence helps them worry less." | "Pebble reduces anxiety." |
| "A tool for reflection." | "Therapy in your pocket." |
| "Your data stays on your device." | "Military-grade security." |
| "Free and open source." | "The best journaling app." |

## Ethical commitments
1. **No dark patterns** ([Design system](../03-design/design-system.md), [Calm Audit](../05-testing/calm-audit.md)).
2. **No monetising data.** There is no data to monetise, by design.
3. **No engagement maximising.** We don't optimise for time-in-app. A user who stops needing Pebble is a success.
4. **Inclusive by default:** accessible, free, works on cheap phones, no account barrier.
5. **Honest limits:** when something doesn't work or isn't known, say so.

## If Pebble is ever studied
If a researcher wants to evaluate Pebble, welcome it. It must go through their institution's
ethics review; Pebble itself never adds data collection. Results, positive or negative, are
linked from the README.

## Done when
Every public sentence about Pebble passes the claims test.
