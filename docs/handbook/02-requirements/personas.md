# Personas
*Phase: Requirements · Who we design for. Every story names a persona.*

Personas are fictional but realistic composites. They stop us designing for "everyone", which
means designing for no one.

---

## Asha, 24: the quiet overthinker *(primary)*
**Situation:** Junior designer. Replays conversations at night. Expects the worst at work. Has never
seen a therapist and wouldn't call herself "anxious".
**Goals:** Feel lighter at night. Stop the same worries looping.
**Frustrations:** Journaling apps feel like homework; mental-health apps feel clinical and make her
feel "diagnosed"; she doesn't want her thoughts on a server.
**What Pebble must do for her:** capture in under 20 seconds; never feel like therapy; no guilt
when she skips a week; proof, over time, that her forecasts are often wrong.
**Quote:** *"I don't want to talk about my feelings. I just want my brain to be quieter."*

## Leo, 35: the data person *(secondary)*
**Situation:** Engineer. Distrusts "wellness" language; trusts numbers.
**Goals:** See an honest tally. Export to a spreadsheet.
**Frustrations:** Vague "insights", gamification, emojis.
**What Pebble must do for him:** a clear count in the Garden; raw JSON export; no fluff.
**Quote:** *"Show me the hit rate."*

## Mira, 29: in therapy *(secondary)*
**Situation:** Seeing a CBT therapist who assigned a worry log. Paper logs get lost.
**Goals:** Bring an organised log to sessions.
**Frustrations:** Apps that don't export; apps that over-interpret.
**What Pebble must do for her:** dated, ordered records; printable summary (v1.x).
**Quote:** *"My therapist asked if any of last week's worries came true. I couldn't remember."*

## Anti-persona: who we don't design for
**Sam, in acute crisis.** Pebble is not the right tool, and it must say so kindly and point to real
help ([Safety](../08-safety-ethics/safety.md)). We never optimise features for crisis use, because
that would imply Pebble can handle it.

## Accessibility lens (applies to all personas)
Design as if any persona might also: use a screen reader; use only a keyboard; have low vision
(200% zoom); be sensitive to motion; be tired, distracted or upset. Pebble is often used at night
and in low moods. **Assume a tired user.**

## Done when
Every user story begins with one of these personas.
