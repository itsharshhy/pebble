# Pebble 🪨
**A calm, private place to set your worries down, and see how many of them actually happened.**

You write a worry in one line. You choose a day to check back. On that day Pebble asks one
gentle question: *Did it happen?* Over time you build your own quiet record of how often
your mind's forecasts came true.

> In a study that tracked worries this way, **91.4% did not come true**, and people who saw that
> more clearly improved more (LaFreniere & Newman, 2020; small clinical sample).

## Why Pebble is different
- **Private:** no account, no server, no tracking. Your words never leave your device.
- **Calm:** no streaks, no badges, no red, no "you missed 5 days". Ever.
- **Evidence-based:** every feature cites its research and how strong that research is. See the [Handbook](docs/HANDBOOK.md#2-the-science).
- **Free and open source** (MIT).
