All notable changes, written for the people who use Pebble. Newest first.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-12 (not released yet)
### Added
- Pebble as a desktop app for Windows, macOS and Linux.
- Set a worry down in one line, choose when to look back, and answer "Did it happen?" on that day.
- "Let it go" for any pebble you'd rather not revisit, and Undo for eight seconds after answering.
- Your garden: an honest count of how many checked pebbles didn't happen.
- Optional feeling word, one small next step, and a breath pause before looking back.
- Day and evening palettes that follow your system, and a calmer experience when your system asks for reduced motion.
- Export and import backups through your system's Save and Open windows; delete everything in one confirmed step.
- Your pebbles are saved in a file on your computer, safely: a crash can't leave half a file, and the previous save is always kept.
